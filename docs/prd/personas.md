# PRD — Personas

Status: TODO

One section per persona. A persona that never appears in a user story or the permissions
matrix is decoration — delete it.

## <Persona name> — <role in one phrase>

- **Context:** who they are, where and on what device they use this
- **Technical level:** what you can assume they know
- **Needs:** what they come here to do
- **Pain:** what frustrates them today
- **Success:** what makes this worth their time
- **Access level:** maps to which role in `../tech/permissions-matrix.md`

## Anti-persona (optional)

Who this is explicitly **not** for. Useful for saying no to feature requests later.
